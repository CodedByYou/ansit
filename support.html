Common questions

"Who's reciting?" didn't name the reciter. Hold the phone closer to the sound for 5–10 seconds. Recognition works best with a clear recitation and little background noise. If the reciter isn't in Ansit yet, it still shows where in the Quran the recitation is.

Prayer times look wrong. Open the prayer screen and check the city at the bottom. Set it with "Use my location" or search for your city. The calculation method and Asr can be changed there too.

Reminders don't arrive. Allow notifications for Ansit in your device's settings, and on Android allow alarms and reminders.

The microphone isn't working. Allow the microphone for Ansit in your device's settings, under Privacy.

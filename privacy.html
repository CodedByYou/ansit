Privacy policy

Ansit (أنصت) · effective 10 October 2026
In short: Ansit has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect personal data. Your voice is recognised on your device and never uploaded.

What stays on your device

Your settings, bookmarks, reading and listening progress, khatmah, hifz progress, reminders, downloaded recitations and your own imported recordings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Microphone

Ansit uses the microphone only when you start a listening feature: naming the reciter of a recitation playing nearby ("Who's reciting?"), finding the ayah you recite, hifz practice and live captions. The audio is processed on your device and is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Location

Location is optional and only used to set the city for prayer times and the Qibla. Ansit rounds your position to about a kilometre and asks the operating system's geocoder (Apple's on iPhone, iPad and Mac; Google's on Android) for the city's name. You can search for your city instead and never share your location.

Content the app downloads

To play and show content, Ansit requests it from content services, including Quran Foundation (translations and tafsir), recitation hosts such as mp3quran.net, the Haramain's public feeds, Pexels (background images) and YouTube (embedded videos). Like any web request, these services can see your device's IP address. Ansit sends them no personal information, and they are governed by their own privacy policies.

Notifications

Prayer, adhkar and other reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification server is involved.

Live captions translation (optional)

If you add your own API key for a translation provider (Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic), only the text of the khutbah's words is sent to that provider to be translated, never audio. Your key is stored in your device's keychain. Without a key, translation happens on your device.

Diagnostics

Crash and performance reports are kept on your device. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple or Google may provide us anonymous crash and performance data under their own terms.

Children

Ansit is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
